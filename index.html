LexCopilot

A legal assistant that answers from your own PDFs, fully offline.

  • C#
  • .NET
  • WPF
  • Semantic Kernel

Code ↗

  • Every claim in an answer points to the article it came from. If the documents don't cover the question, it tells you so instead of making up a law.
  • Embeddings, search and the model all run on your machine: nomic-embed-text, cosine similarity I wrote by hand, Llama 3 through Ollama.

HelpMate

An AI interviewer that asks technical questions and grades your answers.

  • You pick a role and a level and answer in your own words. You get a score out of ten and a note on what was missing.
  • Nobody can talk the AI into reading another user's data.
  • Its 3 tools never receive a user id. The server attaches it from the login token, so a prompt injection has nothing to hijack.
  • Claude runs it in production, and a free local model runs it offline through Ollama. The second provider took 100 lines and left the interview logic alone.
  • .NET 10
  • ASP.NET Core
  • Blazor
  • EF Core
  • Anthropic API / Ollama
  • xUnit

SmartifyBot

A 6-axis robot arm that finds plastic bottles with a camera and follows voice commands.

  • I built all of it alone. A Teensy 4.1 handles the inverse kinematics and an Arduino Mega drives the gripper.
  • The two boards talk over a serial protocol I designed. Vision runs on OpenCV with a YOLO detector, and a Python control panel sits on top.
  • The head comes off. Swap in a gripper, a 3D-printing head or a laser.
  • It won three national awards in 2024: 1st at InfoEducatie, 2nd at ONCS and 2nd at RoSEF.
  • Python (OpenCV, YOLO, SpeechRecognition)
  • C++ (Arduino, Teensy)
  • Fusion 360
  • Unity
